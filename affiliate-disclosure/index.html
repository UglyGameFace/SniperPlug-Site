Affiliate disclosure

Some approved links may earn SniperPlug a commission.

When you click an eligible retailer link and complete a qualifying purchase, SniperPlug may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

Effective July 31, 2026

How monetized links are identified

Eligible deal buttons may use rel="sponsored nofollow" and may pass through a SniperPlug redirect before opening an official retailer or approved affiliate deep link. A redirect does not make SniperPlug the seller and does not change the retailer’s price, checkout, returns, or customer-service terms.

Editorial and verification independence

Affiliate eligibility does not make an offer accurate. SniperPlug’s publication standard requires an exact product destination and sufficient offer context before a deal card can be published. We do not publish placeholder prices or broad retailer searches as deals merely because a link could earn commission.

Price and availability

Retail prices, coupons, rewards, seller offers, inventory, shipping, pickup, membership eligibility, and checkout totals can change quickly and may vary by account or location. Always verify the final product, seller, condition, price, and terms on the retailer’s official page.

Retailer relationships

SniperPlug is independent. Use of a retailer’s name or trademark describes the destination or coverage area and does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, ownership, or an approved affiliate relationship unless SniperPlug expressly states that the relationship is active.

Current and future programs

SniperPlug may participate in more than one retailer or affiliate-network program. Approval, commission rates, eligible products, attribution windows, and program terms can change. We will not label a retailer relationship as approved until it is actually approved.

Questions or corrections

Send questions about monetized links, inaccurate destinations, or retailer relationships to partners@sniperplug.com. Deal corrections may also be sent to support@sniperplug.com.
