How SniperPlug works

Useful alerts without hiding the fine print.

The platform is designed to make retail offers easier to understand—not to impersonate a retailer or guarantee inventory that can change after detection.

01

Discover

Monitor supported retail sources for meaningful price changes, promotions, availability signals, and clearance opportunities.

02

Check context

Preserve the product variant, seller, fulfillment method, location sensitivity, and visible offer terms whenever the source provides them.

03

Send shoppers safely

Present the alert clearly and direct the shopper to the official retailer page for the final price, eligibility, inventory, and checkout.
