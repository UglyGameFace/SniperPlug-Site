How SniperPlug works

Three checks before a deal earns a public card.

A discount is only useful when you can tell exactly what it applies to and where the verified offer lives.

01

Find the signal

SniperPlug identifies a meaningful price, promotion, clearance, availability, or restock signal from a supported source.

02

Match the exact offer

Product, variant, seller, condition, fulfillment, location assumptions, and visible offer terms are checked before publication.

03

Send you to the source

The public card points to the exact official product destination so you can confirm final price, inventory, and eligibility before buying.
