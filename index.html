How SniperPlug works

Useful alerts without hiding the fine print.

The platform is designed to make retail offers easier to understand, not to impersonate a retailer or guarantee inventory that can change.

01

Discover

Identify meaningful product, promotion, price, and availability signals from supported sources.

02

Verify context

Confirm the exact product, variant, seller, condition, fulfillment, location assumptions, and visible offer terms.

03

Publish responsibly

Publish only with an official product destination and remind shoppers to verify final terms at the retailer.
