The hard rule

Exact destination or no card.

A future card must identify the retailer, exact product or SKU, important variant details, visible offer evidence, seller or fulfillment context when relevant, verification time, and the official product-page destination.

Before a card goes live

Four things SniperPlug checks.

The goal is simple: when a card appears, you should understand what the offer is and where it was verified before leaving SniperPlug.

1

Exact product

The model, size, color, edition, pack, and other offer-defining variants must match the destination.

2

Offer context

Seller, fulfillment, pickup, account eligibility, coupons, rewards, and location sensitivity are kept with the offer when they matter.

3

Official destination

The action must lead to the relevant retailer product page or an approved exact deep link, not a broad search page.

4

Final reality check

Price, stock, terms, and checkout totals can change. The retailer page remains the final source of truth before purchase.
