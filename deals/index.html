Deal verification standard

What SniperPlug checks before publishing.

The standard is intentionally stricter than posting a search result or repeating a headline.

1

Exact product

The title, model, size, color, edition, pack, and other offer-defining variants must match the destination.

2

Offer context

Seller, fulfillment, pickup, account eligibility, coupons, rewards, and location sensitivity are labeled when they affect the final offer.

3

Official destination

The action must lead to the relevant retailer product page or an approved exact deep link—not a broad search page.

4

Final verification

Shoppers are reminded to confirm price, stock, terms, and checkout totals on the retailer’s site because retail conditions can change quickly.
