About SniperPlug

Deal discovery with exact-offer discipline.

SniperPlug is an independent retail publishing and alert platform designed to help shoppers understand an offer before leaving for the retailer’s official page.

What we publish

Verified records can include the exact product or SKU, variant, visible price evidence, seller or condition, fulfillment, promotion terms, location assumptions, verification time, and official product destination.

What we reject

Placeholder prices, invented discounts, mismatched variants, unsupported availability claims, generic retailer searches presented as product links, and claims of affiliation that do not exist.

◎

Independent publisher

↗

Official destinations

Future deal actions must lead to exact retailer product pages or approved deep links.

Affiliate relationships are disclosed and never treated as proof that an offer is accurate.
