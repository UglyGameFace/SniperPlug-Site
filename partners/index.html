Partner-readiness checklist

  • Public contact, privacy, terms, and affiliate-disclosure pages are available sitewide.
  • Retailer trademarks are used descriptively without claiming ownership or endorsement.
  • Offer pages direct shoppers to official retailer destinations for final verification and checkout.
  • Location, account, coupon, membership, seller, condition, and fulfillment limitations are disclosed when relevant.
  • Placeholder prices and broad search-result click-outs are not accepted as live deals.
  • Authorized APIs, catalogs, feeds, and rate limits are handled according to the partner’s requirements.
